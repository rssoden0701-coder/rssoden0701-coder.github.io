OUR PROMISE

Three things we hold to

01

Chosen with care

Motobu Wagyu and Miyako Beef from Okinawa, Aso Akaushi from Kumamoto. We select by breed, cut and grade, and serve only what convinces us.

02

Yakiniku, without the work

Teppan yakiniku: the meat is grilled for you, in front of you, on iron and hot stone. No smoke, no tongs — just tell us how you like it cooked.

03

8 counter seats + 2 private rooms

Eight counter seats and two private rooms for four. A quiet place, whether you are alone or with someone who matters.

A heritage chicken once lost and brought back by local breeders. Firm, and it gets better the longer you chew.
